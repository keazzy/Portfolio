Halvest is a halal investment platform helping Muslims grow wealth ethically. When I joined, the product was web-only and retention was a challenge.

I was tasked with designing a mobile app that would make ethical investing accessible to a broader audience while maintaining the trust and credibility the platform had built.

Making ethical investing mobile-first

The web platform served its purpose, but user research revealed a clear pattern: potential investors discovered Halvest on mobile, then bounced when they couldn't complete signup easily. The friction between interest and action was costing conversions.

The mobile app needed to do three things: simplify onboarding, make the investment process intuitive, and maintain the educational depth that helped users understand halal investing. I started with the signup flow, the biggest drop-off point.

Redesigning onboarding for conversion

The original web onboarding was thorough but overwhelming. Regulatory requirements meant collecting significant personal information, and users often abandoned mid-flow. I restructured the experience into digestible steps with clear progress indicators.

Each screen focused on one task. Copy was rewritten to explain why information was needed. The result: onboarding completion rates improved significantly, with users reporting the process felt "way easier" in testing.

Building the investment experience

The core investment flow needed to balance simplicity with the transparency halal investors expect. Users want to understand where their money goes and why it's compliant. I designed portfolio views that surface compliance information without overwhelming the primary action: investing.

The wallet system was redesigned to make deposits and withdrawals intuitive. Transaction history became scannable. And throughout, I maintained visual consistency with the web platform so existing users felt at home.

The impact was immediate. Halvest added 600 new investors in 2025 alone, more than doubling the user base. User testing confirmed what the metrics showed: "Onboarding is way easier."

More importantly, easier access drove business growth. Revenue increased 145% and AUM grew 115% year-over-year, directly tied to mobile accessibility removing the barrier between interest and investment. The mobile app proved that ethical investing didn't need to be complicated to be credible.

We've continued iterating on the mobile experience, refining flows based on user behavior. Our north star remains unchanged: making ethical investing easy for everyone.
