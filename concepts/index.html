Same across all three (and what your editor will control): your intro and summary, work history, companies (logo, role, dates, summary, impact numbers) and shots. Aurora curtain at the top of each: a WebGL port of Nathan Schroeder’s aurora-curtain shader, recoloured per concept: warm Ember light on A’s paper (Iris and Claret via its accent switcher), the original violet on B’s dark canvas (sage in light mode), and pale daylight on C’s sage.
